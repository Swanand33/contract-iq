# Independent Contractor Agreement

**Agreement Number:** VP-ICA-2024-0034  
**Effective Date:** May 1, 2024  
**Parties:**  
- **Company:** Velocity Payments Inc., 200 Fintech Tower, 12th Floor, New York, NY 10004  
- **Contractor:** Alex Nguyen, doing business as Nguyen Digital Consulting  

---

## 1. Engagement

1.1 Company engages Contractor as an independent contractor to perform the services described in Exhibit A (the "Services"). Contractor is not an employee of Company and nothing in this Agreement shall create an employment, partnership, or joint venture relationship.

1.2 The initial engagement period is six (6) months from the Effective Date (the "Initial Term"), unless terminated earlier in accordance with Section 8.

---

## 2. Services

2.1 Contractor shall perform the Services described in Exhibit A, which includes: (a) backend API development for payment processing integrations; (b) database optimization and query performance tuning; (c) integration testing with third-party payment gateways; and (d) technical documentation for developed components.

2.2 Contractor shall perform the Services remotely, with occasional on-site visits to Company's offices as mutually agreed, not to exceed four (4) days per month.

2.3 Contractor shall dedicate approximately thirty (30) hours per week to the Services, with flexibility in scheduling. Contractor may provide services to other clients, provided such work does not create a conflict of interest or interfere with Contractor's obligations under this Agreement.

---

## 3. Compensation

3.1 Company shall pay Contractor at the rate specified in Exhibit A. Payment shall be made within fifteen (15) days of receipt of Contractor's monthly invoice.

3.2 Contractor shall submit invoices on the first business day of each month for Services performed in the preceding month. Invoices shall include a summary of hours worked and tasks completed.

3.3 Contractor is responsible for all taxes, including self-employment taxes, arising from compensation under this Agreement. Company shall issue a Form 1099 to Contractor as required by law.

3.4 Reasonable pre-approved expenses (travel, software licenses, and hosting costs directly related to the Services) shall be reimbursed by Company upon submission of receipts.

---

## 4. Deliverables and Acceptance

4.1 Contractor shall deliver the work product described in Exhibit A according to the milestone schedule set forth therein.

4.2 Company shall review each deliverable within ten (10) business days and provide written acceptance or a list of required changes. Contractor shall address required changes within five (5) business days.

4.3 Deliverables shall be considered accepted if Company does not provide written feedback within the review period.

---

## 5. Intellectual Property

5.1 **Work Product.** All work product created by Contractor in the course of performing the Services, including source code, documentation, designs, and related materials ("Work Product"), shall be the sole property of Company.

5.2 **Assignment.** Contractor hereby irrevocably assigns to Company all right, title, and interest in and to the Work Product, including all intellectual property rights therein. Contractor shall execute any documents necessary to perfect Company's ownership.

5.3 **Pre-Existing Materials.** Contractor retains ownership of any pre-existing tools, libraries, or frameworks owned by Contractor prior to this engagement ("Pre-Existing Materials"). To the extent any Work Product incorporates Pre-Existing Materials, Contractor grants Company a non-exclusive, perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free license to use such Pre-Existing Materials solely as part of the Work Product.

5.4 **Open Source.** Contractor shall not incorporate any open-source software into the Work Product without Company's prior written approval. Contractor shall identify any open-source components and their licenses for Company's review.

---

## 6. Confidentiality

6.1 **Confidential Information.** Contractor acknowledges that in performing the Services, Contractor will have access to Company's Confidential Information, including payment processing algorithms, transaction data structures, customer financial data, API keys, security protocols, business strategies, and unreleased product features.

6.2 **Obligations.** Contractor shall: (a) hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence; (b) use Confidential Information solely for performing the Services; (c) not disclose Confidential Information to any third party; and (d) take all reasonable precautions to prevent unauthorized disclosure, including encrypting all devices and communications used for the Services.

6.3 **PCI-DSS Compliance.** Contractor acknowledges that Company's systems process payment card data subject to the Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard ("PCI-DSS"). Contractor shall comply with all applicable PCI-DSS requirements in performing the Services and shall not store, process, or transmit cardholder data outside of Company's approved systems.

6.4 **Return of Materials.** Upon termination, Contractor shall immediately return or destroy all Company materials and Confidential Information, delete all copies from personal devices, and certify completion in writing.

6.5 Confidentiality obligations shall survive termination indefinitely for trade secrets and for five (5) years for other Confidential Information.

---

## 7. Representations and Warranties

7.1 Contractor represents and warrants that: (a) Contractor has the skills, experience, and qualifications necessary to perform the Services; (b) the Services and Work Product will be original and will not infringe any third-party rights; (c) Contractor is legally authorized to work and has obtained any necessary business licenses; and (d) Contractor's performance of the Services does not violate any agreement with any third party.

7.2 THE SERVICES ARE PROVIDED WITHOUT WARRANTIES OF MERCHANTABILITY OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE BEYOND THOSE EXPRESSLY STATED IN THIS SECTION.

---

## 8. Termination

8.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement upon fourteen (14) days' written notice for any reason.

8.2 Company may terminate this Agreement immediately if Contractor: (a) breaches Section 6 (Confidentiality); (b) breaches Section 5.4 (Open Source restrictions); or (c) fails to perform the Services in a professional manner after written notice and a seven (7) day cure period.

8.3 Upon termination, Company shall pay Contractor for all Services performed and accepted through the effective date of termination.

8.4 Sections 5, 6, 7, 9, and 10 shall survive termination of this Agreement.

---

## 9. Non-Solicitation

9.1 During the term of this Agreement and for six (6) months thereafter, Contractor shall not directly or indirectly solicit, recruit, or hire any employee of Company with whom Contractor has had material contact during the engagement.

---

## 10. Indemnification

10.1 Contractor shall indemnify Company against third-party claims arising from: (a) Contractor's negligence or willful misconduct; (b) Contractor's misclassification as an employee for tax purposes; or (c) infringement of third-party IP rights by the Work Product.

10.2 Company shall indemnify Contractor against claims arising from Company's use of the Work Product in violation of applicable law.

---

## 11. Limitation of Liability

11.1 NEITHER PARTY'S LIABILITY SHALL EXCEED THE TOTAL COMPENSATION PAID UNDER THIS AGREEMENT DURING THE SIX (6) MONTHS PRECEDING THE CLAIM.

11.2 NEITHER PARTY SHALL BE LIABLE FOR INDIRECT OR CONSEQUENTIAL DAMAGES.

---

## 12. General Provisions

12.1 **Governing Law.** This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of New York.

12.2 **Independent Contractor Status.** Contractor is an independent contractor and shall not be entitled to employee benefits. Contractor controls the manner and means of performing the Services.

12.3 **Entire Agreement.** This Agreement and its Exhibits constitute the entire agreement between the parties.

12.4 **Notices.** Notices shall be sent to the addresses above or by email to the contacts specified in Exhibit A.

# Employment Agreement

**Agreement Number:** SL-EMP-2024-0078  
**Effective Date:** June 1, 2024  
**Parties:**  
- **Employer:** SilverLine Logistics Inc., 8800 Commerce Drive, Chicago, IL 60607  
- **Employee:** Jordan Rivera  

---

## 1. Position and Duties

1.1 Employer hereby employs Employee as **Senior Data Engineer**, reporting to the Vice President of Engineering.

1.2 Employee's primary responsibilities shall include: (a) designing and implementing data pipelines and ETL processes; (b) managing cloud data infrastructure on AWS; (c) collaborating with analytics and machine learning teams on data requirements; (d) mentoring junior engineers; and (e) such other duties as may reasonably be assigned by Employer from time to time.

1.3 Employee shall devote full working time and best efforts to the performance of duties under this Agreement. Employee shall not engage in any other employment or consulting activity without Employer's prior written consent, except for passive investment activities or board service disclosed to and approved by Employer.

---

## 2. Compensation

2.1 **Base Salary.** Employer shall pay Employee an annual base salary, payable in accordance with Employer's standard payroll schedule (bi-weekly), subject to applicable withholdings and deductions.

2.2 **Annual Bonus.** Employee shall be eligible for an annual performance bonus of up to twenty percent (20%) of base salary, based on individual performance and company financial results, payable within ninety (90) days of the fiscal year end.

2.3 **Equity.** Employee shall be granted stock options as specified in a separate Stock Option Agreement, subject to a four (4) year vesting schedule with a one (1) year cliff.

2.4 Compensation may be reviewed annually and adjusted at Employer's discretion, but shall not be reduced without Employee's written consent.

---

## 3. Benefits

3.1 Employee shall be entitled to participate in Employer's standard benefits programs, including: (a) health, dental, and vision insurance; (b) 401(k) retirement plan with employer matching; (c) life and disability insurance; and (d) any other benefits made generally available to similarly situated employees.

3.2 Employee shall receive twenty (20) days of paid time off (PTO) per calendar year, accruing on a pro-rata basis. Unused PTO may be carried over up to a maximum of ten (10) days.

3.3 Employee shall be eligible for Employer's professional development benefit of up to five thousand dollars ($5,000) per year for conferences, training, and certifications.

---

## 4. Confidentiality

4.1 Employee acknowledges that during the course of employment, Employee will have access to and become familiar with Confidential Information of Employer. "Confidential Information" includes, without limitation: trade secrets, business strategies, customer lists, pricing information, financial data, proprietary technology, source code, algorithms, product roadmaps, and employee information.

4.2 Employee shall not, during or after employment, directly or indirectly disclose, publish, or use any Confidential Information for any purpose other than the performance of Employee's duties, without Employer's prior written consent.

4.3 Upon termination of employment, Employee shall immediately return to Employer all documents, files, devices, and materials containing Confidential Information.

4.4 Nothing in this Section 4 shall prevent Employee from making disclosures protected under whistleblower statutes or as required by law or court order.

---

## 5. Intellectual Property

5.1 **Work Product Assignment.** All inventions, discoveries, improvements, original works of authorship, software, and other intellectual property conceived or developed by Employee within the scope of employment or using Employer's resources ("Work Product") shall be the sole and exclusive property of Employer.

5.2 Employee hereby assigns to Employer all right, title, and interest in any Work Product. Employee shall execute any documents and take any actions reasonably necessary to perfect Employer's ownership of Work Product.

5.3 **Prior Inventions.** Employee has disclosed in Exhibit A any inventions or intellectual property created prior to employment that Employee wishes to exclude from this assignment. If no Exhibit A is attached, Employee represents that no such prior inventions exist.

5.4 This Section 5 does not apply to inventions developed entirely on Employee's own time, without use of Employer's resources, and that do not relate to Employer's current or anticipated business.

---

## 6. Non-Competition

6.1 During employment and for a period of twelve (12) months following termination of employment for any reason, Employee shall not, directly or indirectly, engage in, own, manage, or provide services to any business that competes with Employer's logistics technology and data analytics business within the United States.

6.2 This restriction applies to employment, consulting, advisory, or board roles with any Competing Business. "Competing Business" means any entity that derives more than twenty-five percent (25%) of its revenue from logistics data analytics or supply chain optimization technology.

6.3 Employee acknowledges that this restriction is reasonable in scope, duration, and geographic area and is necessary to protect Employer's legitimate business interests.

6.4 If any court determines that this Section 6 is overly broad, the court may modify the restriction to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable.

---

## 7. Non-Solicitation

7.1 During employment and for twelve (12) months thereafter, Employee shall not directly or indirectly: (a) solicit, recruit, or hire any employee of Employer; or (b) solicit or divert any customer, client, or business partner of Employer for the benefit of a Competing Business.

7.2 This restriction does not apply to general advertising or recruitment efforts not specifically targeted at Employer's employees.

---

## 8. Termination

8.1 **At-Will Employment.** Employment under this Agreement is at-will. Either party may terminate employment at any time, with or without cause, upon written notice.

8.2 **Termination by Employer Without Cause.** If Employer terminates Employee's employment without Cause, Employee shall be entitled to: (a) continued payment of base salary for six (6) months; (b) payment of any earned but unpaid bonus; and (c) COBRA premium reimbursement for six (6) months, subject to Employee executing a release agreement within twenty-one (21) days.

8.3 **Termination for Cause.** "Cause" means: (a) material breach of this Agreement; (b) conviction of a felony or crime of moral turpitude; (c) willful misconduct or gross negligence; (d) fraud or dishonesty in connection with employment; or (e) continued failure to perform duties after written notice and a thirty (30) day cure period.

8.4 **Resignation.** Employee may resign upon thirty (30) days' written notice. Employer may waive all or part of the notice period and accelerate the termination date.

8.5 Upon termination, Employee shall cooperate with Employer in transitioning responsibilities and shall comply with Section 4 (Confidentiality) and Section 5 (Intellectual Property).

---

## 9. Dispute Resolution

9.1 Any dispute arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall first be submitted to mediation administered by a mutually agreed mediator.

9.2 If mediation does not resolve the dispute within forty-five (45) days, either party may initiate binding arbitration under the Employment Arbitration Rules of the American Arbitration Association in Chicago, Illinois.

9.3 Each party shall bear its own costs and attorneys' fees unless the arbitrator determines otherwise.

---

## 10. General Provisions

10.1 **Governing Law.** This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Illinois.

10.2 **Entire Agreement.** This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement and supersedes all prior agreements.

10.3 **Severability.** If any provision is held invalid, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force.

10.4 **Amendment.** This Agreement may be amended only in writing signed by both parties.

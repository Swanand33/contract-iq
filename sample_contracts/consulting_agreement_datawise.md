# Professional Services Consulting Agreement

**Agreement Number:** DW-PSA-2024-0156  
**Effective Date:** February 10, 2024  
**Parties:**  
- **Client:** Horizon Health Systems Inc., 3500 Medical Center Drive, Nashville, TN 37203  
- **Consultant:** DataWise Consulting LLC, 775 Technology Park, Suite 400, Boulder, CO 80301  

---

## 1. Definitions

1.1 **"Services"** means the professional consulting services described in one or more Statements of Work executed pursuant to this Agreement.

1.2 **"Statement of Work" or "SOW"** means a document that describes the specific Services to be performed, deliverables, timeline, and fees, and that is executed by both parties and attached hereto.

1.3 **"Deliverables"** means the work product, reports, analyses, software, documentation, or other materials to be produced by Consultant as specified in the applicable SOW.

1.4 **"Client Materials"** means any data, documents, systems, or information provided by Client to Consultant for use in performing the Services.

1.5 **"Background IP"** means any intellectual property owned by or licensed to a party prior to the Effective Date or developed independently of the Services.

---

## 2. Scope of Services

2.1 Consultant shall perform the Services described in each SOW in a professional and workmanlike manner consistent with applicable industry standards.

2.2 Consultant shall assign qualified personnel to perform the Services. Client may request replacement of any Consultant personnel who fail to meet reasonable performance standards, and Consultant shall use commercially reasonable efforts to provide a suitable replacement within ten (10) business days.

2.3 Unless otherwise specified in the SOW, Consultant shall perform the Services at its own facilities. Any on-site work at Client's facilities shall be coordinated in advance.

2.4 Changes to the scope of Services described in a SOW shall be documented in a written Change Order signed by both parties. No additional fees shall be incurred without Client's prior written approval.

---

## 3. Fees and Expenses

3.1 Client shall pay Consultant the fees specified in the applicable SOW. Unless otherwise stated, fees are calculated on a time-and-materials basis at the hourly rates set forth in Exhibit B.

3.2 Consultant shall submit monthly invoices detailing hours worked, tasks performed, and any pre-approved expenses. Client shall pay each invoice within thirty (30) days of receipt.

3.3 Reasonable travel, lodging, and out-of-pocket expenses incurred by Consultant in performing the Services shall be reimbursed by Client, provided such expenses are pre-approved in writing and documented with receipts.

3.4 All fees are exclusive of applicable taxes. Client shall be responsible for any sales, use, or value-added taxes imposed on the Services, excluding taxes based on Consultant's income.

---

## 4. Intellectual Property Rights

4.1 **Client Ownership of Deliverables.** Subject to Section 4.2, all Deliverables created by Consultant specifically for Client under this Agreement shall be considered "work made for hire" to the extent permitted by law. To the extent any Deliverable does not qualify as work made for hire, Consultant hereby assigns to Client all right, title, and interest in and to such Deliverable.

4.2 **Consultant Background IP.** Consultant retains all right, title, and interest in its Background IP. To the extent any Deliverable incorporates Consultant's Background IP, Consultant grants Client a non-exclusive, perpetual, royalty-free license to use such Background IP solely as embedded in the Deliverable.

4.3 **Client Materials.** Client retains all right, title, and interest in Client Materials. Consultant shall use Client Materials solely for the purpose of performing the Services.

---

## 5. Confidentiality

5.1 Each party shall maintain the confidentiality of the other party's Confidential Information using the same degree of care it uses for its own confidential information, but no less than reasonable care.

5.2 "Confidential Information" includes, without limitation: business strategies, financial information, customer data, patient health information, proprietary software, trade secrets, and any information marked or identified as confidential.

5.3 Consultant acknowledges that Client's data may include Protected Health Information ("PHI") as defined under the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act ("HIPAA"). Consultant agrees to execute a Business Associate Agreement ("BAA") as required and to comply with all applicable HIPAA requirements.

5.4 The confidentiality obligations shall survive termination of this Agreement for a period of five (5) years, except with respect to trade secrets, which shall be protected indefinitely.

---

## 6. Representations and Warranties

6.1 Consultant represents and warrants that: (a) it has the requisite skills, experience, and qualifications to perform the Services; (b) the Services will be performed in a professional manner consistent with industry standards; (c) the Deliverables will conform to the specifications set forth in the applicable SOW; and (d) the Deliverables will not infringe any third-party intellectual property rights.

6.2 Client represents and warrants that it has the authority to provide Client Materials to Consultant and that the use of Client Materials by Consultant as contemplated by this Agreement will not violate any applicable law or third-party right.

6.3 EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY SET FORTH IN THIS AGREEMENT, NEITHER PARTY MAKES ANY WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING WARRANTIES OF MERCHANTABILITY OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

---

## 7. Limitation of Liability

7.1 CONSULTANT'S TOTAL LIABILITY UNDER THIS AGREEMENT SHALL NOT EXCEED THE TOTAL FEES PAID BY CLIENT TO CONSULTANT UNDER THE APPLICABLE SOW DURING THE SIX (6) MONTH PERIOD PRECEDING THE CLAIM.

7.2 IN NO EVENT SHALL EITHER PARTY BE LIABLE FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, SPECIAL, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES ARISING OUT OF OR RELATED TO THIS AGREEMENT.

7.3 The limitations in this Section 7 shall not apply to breaches of confidentiality obligations or Consultant's indemnification obligations under Section 8.

---

## 8. Indemnification

8.1 Consultant shall indemnify and hold harmless Client from any third-party claims, damages, and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising from: (a) Consultant's gross negligence or willful misconduct in performing the Services; (b) any claim that the Deliverables infringe a third-party's intellectual property rights; or (c) Consultant's breach of its confidentiality obligations.

8.2 Client shall indemnify and hold harmless Consultant from any third-party claims arising from Client's use of the Deliverables in a manner not contemplated by this Agreement.

---

## 9. Term and Termination

9.1 This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue until all SOWs have been completed or this Agreement is terminated in accordance with this Section 9.

9.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement or any SOW upon thirty (30) days' written notice if the other party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure such breach within the notice period.

9.3 Client may terminate any SOW for convenience upon fifteen (15) days' written notice. In such event, Client shall pay Consultant for all Services performed and expenses incurred through the effective date of termination, plus any non-cancellable commitments made by Consultant in reliance on the SOW.

9.4 Sections 4, 5, 7, 8, and 10 shall survive termination of this Agreement.

---

## 10. General Provisions

10.1 **Independent Contractor.** Consultant is an independent contractor and not an employee, agent, or partner of Client. Consultant is responsible for its own taxes, insurance, and benefits.

10.2 **Governing Law.** This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Tennessee.

10.3 **Non-Solicitation.** During the term of this Agreement and for twelve (12) months thereafter, neither party shall directly solicit the other party's employees who were involved in performing or receiving the Services without prior written consent.

10.4 **Entire Agreement.** This Agreement, together with all SOWs and Exhibits, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties.

10.5 **Notices.** All notices shall be in writing and sent to the addresses specified above.

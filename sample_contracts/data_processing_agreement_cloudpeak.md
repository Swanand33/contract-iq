# Data Processing Agreement

**Agreement Number:** CP-DPA-2024-0233  
**Effective Date:** April 1, 2024  
**Parties:**  
- **Data Controller:** NovaTech Solutions Corp., 950 Enterprise Way, Seattle, WA 98101  
- **Data Processor:** CloudPeak Infrastructure Inc., 2200 Server Farm Road, Phoenix, AZ 85004  

---

## 1. Definitions

1.1 **"Personal Data"** means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person processed by Data Processor on behalf of Data Controller pursuant to the Principal Agreement.

1.2 **"Principal Agreement"** means the Cloud Infrastructure Services Agreement between the parties dated January 1, 2024 (Agreement No. CP-CISA-2024-0098).

1.3 **"Processing"** means any operation or set of operations performed on Personal Data, including collection, recording, organization, storage, adaptation, alteration, retrieval, consultation, use, disclosure by transmission, dissemination, erasure, or destruction.

1.4 **"Sub-processor"** means any third party engaged by Data Processor to process Personal Data on behalf of Data Controller.

1.5 **"Data Breach"** means any accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure of, or access to Personal Data.

1.6 **"Applicable Data Protection Law"** means all laws and regulations relating to data protection and privacy applicable to the Processing of Personal Data under this Agreement, including the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR"), the California Consumer Privacy Act ("CCPA"), and their implementing regulations.

---

## 2. Scope and Purpose of Processing

2.1 Data Processor shall process Personal Data solely on behalf of Data Controller and only in accordance with Data Controller's documented instructions, unless required to do so by applicable law, in which case Data Processor shall inform Data Controller of such legal requirement before Processing (unless the law prohibits such notification).

2.2 The subject matter of Processing is the provision of cloud infrastructure services as described in the Principal Agreement. The categories of Personal Data and data subjects are described in Annex I.

2.3 The duration of Processing shall be the term of the Principal Agreement unless otherwise agreed in writing.

---

## 3. Data Processor Obligations

3.1 Data Processor shall: (a) process Personal Data only on documented instructions from Data Controller; (b) ensure that persons authorized to process Personal Data are bound by appropriate confidentiality obligations; (c) implement and maintain technical and organizational security measures as described in Annex II; (d) assist Data Controller in fulfilling its obligations to respond to data subject requests; and (e) make available to Data Controller all information necessary to demonstrate compliance with this Agreement.

3.2 Data Processor shall not process Personal Data outside the territories specified in Annex I without Data Controller's prior written consent. Any transfer of Personal Data to a country outside the European Economic Area shall be subject to appropriate safeguards as required by Applicable Data Protection Law.

3.3 Data Processor shall promptly inform Data Controller if, in its opinion, an instruction from Data Controller infringes Applicable Data Protection Law.

---

## 4. Sub-processing

4.1 Data Processor shall not engage any Sub-processor to process Personal Data without Data Controller's prior written authorization. Data Controller hereby provides general authorization for Sub-processors listed in Annex III.

4.2 Data Processor shall notify Data Controller of any intended changes to its Sub-processors at least thirty (30) days in advance, giving Data Controller the opportunity to object to such changes.

4.3 Where Data Processor engages a Sub-processor, it shall impose data protection obligations no less protective than those set forth in this Agreement on the Sub-processor by way of a written contract.

4.4 Data Processor shall remain fully liable to Data Controller for the performance of each Sub-processor's obligations.

---

## 5. Security Measures

5.1 Data Processor shall implement and maintain appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk, including: (a) encryption of Personal Data in transit and at rest using AES-256 or equivalent; (b) the ability to ensure ongoing confidentiality, integrity, availability, and resilience of processing systems; (c) the ability to restore the availability and access to Personal Data in a timely manner in the event of a physical or technical incident; and (d) a process for regularly testing, assessing, and evaluating the effectiveness of security measures.

5.2 Data Processor shall maintain SOC 2 Type II certification (or equivalent) and shall provide copies of current audit reports to Data Controller upon request.

5.3 Data Processor shall conduct vulnerability assessments and penetration testing at least annually and remediate any identified vulnerabilities in accordance with industry-standard timelines.

---

## 6. Data Breach Notification

6.1 Data Processor shall notify Data Controller of any Data Breach without undue delay and in no event later than forty-eight (48) hours after becoming aware of the breach.

6.2 Such notification shall include: (a) the nature of the Data Breach, including the categories and approximate number of data subjects and records affected; (b) the likely consequences of the Data Breach; (c) the measures taken or proposed to address the Data Breach; and (d) the contact details of Data Processor's data protection point of contact.

6.3 Data Processor shall cooperate with Data Controller in investigating and remediating the Data Breach and shall take all reasonable steps to mitigate the effects and minimize any damage resulting from the Data Breach.

---

## 7. Data Subject Rights

7.1 Data Processor shall promptly notify Data Controller upon receiving a request from a data subject to exercise their rights under Applicable Data Protection Law, including rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, and objection.

7.2 Data Processor shall assist Data Controller in fulfilling its obligations to respond to such requests, taking into account the nature of the Processing, through appropriate technical and organizational measures.

7.3 Data Processor shall not respond directly to data subject requests unless authorized to do so by Data Controller or required by applicable law.

---

## 8. Audit Rights

8.1 Data Processor shall make available to Data Controller all information necessary to demonstrate compliance with the obligations set forth in this Agreement and shall allow for and contribute to audits, including inspections, conducted by Data Controller or a third-party auditor mandated by Data Controller.

8.2 Audits shall be conducted with at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice and during normal business hours. Data Controller shall ensure that audits do not unreasonably disrupt Data Processor's business operations.

8.3 Data Controller shall bear its own costs in connection with any audit unless the audit reveals a material non-compliance by Data Processor, in which case Data Processor shall bear the reasonable costs of the audit.

---

## 9. Data Retention and Deletion

9.1 Upon termination of the Principal Agreement or upon Data Controller's written request, Data Processor shall, at Data Controller's option, return all Personal Data to Data Controller in a commonly used, machine-readable format or securely delete all Personal Data in its possession within sixty (60) days.

9.2 Data Processor shall certify in writing the completion of deletion. Data Processor may retain copies of Personal Data only to the extent required by applicable law, and such retained data shall remain subject to the obligations of this Agreement.

---

## 10. Liability and Indemnification

10.1 Each party shall be liable for damages caused by Processing that infringes Applicable Data Protection Law in accordance with the liability provisions of the Principal Agreement.

10.2 Data Processor shall indemnify Data Controller for any fines, penalties, or damages arising directly from Data Processor's breach of this Agreement or its failure to comply with Applicable Data Protection Law with respect to its Processing obligations.

---

## 11. Governing Law

11.1 This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Washington, without regard to conflict-of-laws provisions.

11.2 To the extent that the GDPR applies to the Processing, this Agreement shall also be interpreted in accordance with the GDPR.

# Strategic Partnership Agreement

**Agreement Number:** INV-SPA-2024-0341  
**Effective Date:** March 20, 2024  
**Parties:**  
- **Partner A:** InnovateTech Labs Inc., 3200 Research Triangle Park, Durham, NC 27709  
- **Partner B:** BlueWave Digital Solutions LLC, 1450 Startup Avenue, San Jose, CA 95113  

---

## 1. Purpose

1.1 The parties desire to establish a strategic partnership to jointly develop, market, and sell an integrated solution combining InnovateTech's machine learning platform with BlueWave's customer engagement suite (the "Joint Solution").

1.2 This Agreement sets forth the terms governing the parties' collaboration, including development responsibilities, revenue sharing, intellectual property rights, and marketing obligations.

---

## 2. Definitions

2.1 **"Joint Solution"** means the integrated product combining InnovateTech's ML prediction engine and BlueWave's customer engagement platform, branded as "PredictEngage."

2.2 **"Revenue"** means all gross receipts from licensing, subscription, and professional services fees for the Joint Solution, net of refunds, taxes, and payment processing fees.

2.3 **"Territory"** means North America (United States, Canada, and Mexico) for the initial term, with options to expand as described in Section 9.

2.4 **"Joint IP"** means intellectual property created jointly by both parties specifically for the Joint Solution during the term of this Agreement.

---

## 3. Development Responsibilities

3.1 **InnovateTech** shall be responsible for: (a) developing and maintaining the ML prediction engine components; (b) providing API documentation and integration specifications; (c) ensuring the ML components meet agreed performance benchmarks; and (d) providing second-tier technical support for ML-related issues.

3.2 **BlueWave** shall be responsible for: (a) developing and maintaining the customer engagement interface and dashboards; (b) building the integration layer connecting both platforms; (c) user experience design and front-end development; and (d) providing first-tier customer support for end users.

3.3 Each party shall assign a dedicated project manager and at least two (2) full-time engineers to the Joint Solution development effort.

3.4 The parties shall establish a Joint Steering Committee consisting of two (2) representatives from each party. The Committee shall meet monthly to review progress, resolve issues, and approve product roadmap changes.

---

## 4. Revenue Sharing

4.1 Revenue from the Joint Solution shall be distributed as follows: (a) InnovateTech: forty-five percent (45%); (b) BlueWave: forty-five percent (45%); (c) Joint Marketing Fund: ten percent (10%).

4.2 Revenue distribution shall be calculated and settled quarterly, within thirty (30) days of the end of each calendar quarter.

4.3 Each party shall maintain complete and accurate records of all transactions relating to the Joint Solution and shall make such records available for audit by the other party upon reasonable request, not more than once per year.

4.4 Professional services revenue shall be allocated to the party that performs the services, with a five percent (5%) referral fee paid to the other party for any customer introduced.

---

## 5. Intellectual Property

5.1 **Pre-Existing IP.** Each party retains all right, title, and interest in its pre-existing intellectual property. Neither party acquires any ownership interest in the other party's pre-existing IP through this Agreement.

5.2 **Joint IP.** Joint IP shall be owned equally by both parties. Neither party shall license, assign, or encumber Joint IP without the written consent of the other party.

5.3 **Improvements.** Any improvements to a party's pre-existing IP made during the course of this partnership shall remain the sole property of the party whose IP was improved, even if the other party contributed to such improvement.

5.4 **License Grants.** Each party grants the other a non-exclusive, royalty-free license to use its pre-existing IP solely as necessary to develop, market, and support the Joint Solution during the term of this Agreement.

---

## 6. Marketing and Sales

6.1 The parties shall jointly develop a marketing plan within sixty (60) days of the Effective Date. The Joint Marketing Fund shall be used for co-branded campaigns, trade shows, content marketing, and lead generation.

6.2 Both parties may independently sell the Joint Solution through their respective sales channels, subject to the revenue sharing terms in Section 4.

6.3 All marketing materials for the Joint Solution shall feature co-branding and shall be approved by both parties prior to publication.

6.4 Neither party shall make disparaging statements about the other party or the Joint Solution in any marketing, sales, or public communications.

---

## 7. Exclusivity

7.1 During the first twenty-four (24) months of this Agreement, neither party shall enter into a substantially similar partnership with a direct competitor of the other party for the purpose of developing a competing joint solution in the Territory.

7.2 This exclusivity does not restrict either party from independently developing, marketing, or selling its own products or from entering into partnerships in unrelated product categories.

---

## 8. Representations and Warranties

8.1 Each party represents and warrants that: (a) it has full authority to enter into this Agreement; (b) its performance under this Agreement will not violate any other agreement; (c) its pre-existing IP does not infringe any third-party intellectual property rights; and (d) it will comply with all applicable laws in performing its obligations.

---

## 9. Term and Termination

9.1 This Agreement shall have an initial term of three (3) years from the Effective Date and shall automatically renew for additional one (1) year periods unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days prior to the end of the then-current term.

9.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement upon sixty (60) days' written notice if the other party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within the notice period.

9.3 Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately if the other party becomes insolvent, files for bankruptcy, or ceases business operations.

9.4 Upon termination: (a) each party's license to the other's pre-existing IP shall terminate; (b) Joint IP shall continue to be jointly owned; (c) each party shall return the other's Confidential Information; and (d) revenue sharing obligations for existing customers shall continue for twelve (12) months.

---

## 10. Confidentiality

10.1 Each party shall maintain the confidentiality of the other's proprietary information and shall not use such information except as necessary for the purposes of this Agreement.

10.2 Confidentiality obligations shall survive termination for five (5) years.

---

## 11. Limitation of Liability

11.1 NEITHER PARTY SHALL BE LIABLE FOR INDIRECT, CONSEQUENTIAL, INCIDENTAL, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES.

11.2 Each party's total liability shall not exceed the total Revenue distributed to that party during the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.

---

## 12. Governing Law

12.1 This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.

12.2 Disputes shall be resolved through binding arbitration in Wilmington, Delaware.

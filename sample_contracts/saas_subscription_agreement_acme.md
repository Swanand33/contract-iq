## 1. Definitions

1.1 **"Service"** means the Acme CloudOps platform, including all modules, updates, and patches made available to Subscriber during the Subscription Term.

1.2 **"Subscription Term"** means the initial period of twelve (12) months beginning on the Effective Date, and any renewal periods thereafter.

1.3 **"Authorized Users"** means employees, contractors, and agents of Subscriber who are authorized by Subscriber to access and use the Service, up to the number specified in the Order Form.

1.4 **"Subscriber Data"** means all data, content, and information submitted by or on behalf of Subscriber through the Service.

1.5 **"Documentation"** means the user manuals, online help files, and technical documentation provided by Provider for the Service.

1.6 **"Order Form"** means the ordering document executed by both parties specifying the Service tier, number of Authorized Users, fees, and any additional terms.

1.7 **"Service Level Agreement" or "SLA"** means the service availability and performance commitments set forth in Exhibit A.

## 2. Grant of Rights

2.1 Subject to the terms of this Agreement, Provider grants Subscriber a non-exclusive, non-transferable, limited right to access and use the Service during the Subscription Term solely for Subscriber's internal business operations.

2.2 Subscriber may permit Authorized Users to access the Service, provided that Subscriber ensures each Authorized User complies with the terms of this Agreement.

2.3 Subscriber shall not: (a) sublicense, sell, or transfer the Service to any third party; (b) modify, reverse-engineer, or create derivative works of the Service; (c) use the Service to develop a competing product; or (d) exceed the number of Authorized Users specified in the Order Form.

---

## 3. Fees and Payment

3.1 Subscriber shall pay Provider the fees set forth in the Order Form. Unless otherwise specified, fees are due annually in advance within thirty (30) days of invoice date.

3.2 All fees are quoted in United States Dollars and are non-refundable except as expressly provided in this Agreement.

3.3 Late payments shall accrue interest at the rate of 1.5% per month or the maximum rate permitted by law, whichever is lower.

3.4 Provider may increase fees for any renewal term by providing Subscriber with at least sixty (60) days' prior written notice before the end of the then-current term.

---

## 4. Data Ownership and Privacy

4.1 Subscriber retains all right, title, and interest in and to Subscriber Data. Provider acquires no rights in Subscriber Data except the limited rights necessary to provide the Service.

4.2 Provider shall process Subscriber Data in accordance with its Privacy Policy and applicable data protection laws, including but not limited to the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) where applicable.

4.3 Provider shall implement and maintain commercially reasonable administrative, physical, and technical safeguards to protect Subscriber Data from unauthorized access, disclosure, or destruction.

4.4 Upon termination of this Agreement, Provider shall, at Subscriber's written request, return or delete all Subscriber Data within thirty (30) days, except as required by applicable law.

---

## 5. Service Levels and Support

5.1 Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to maintain Service availability of at least 99.5% during each calendar month, measured in accordance with the SLA in Exhibit A.

5.2 Scheduled maintenance windows shall not exceed four (4) hours per month and shall be communicated to Subscriber at least forty-eight (48) hours in advance.

5.3 Provider shall provide technical support during business hours (8:00 AM to 6:00 PM Pacific Time, Monday through Friday) via email and phone. Premium support tiers may be purchased as set forth in the Order Form.

5.4 In the event Provider fails to meet the SLA for three (3) consecutive months, Subscriber may terminate this Agreement without penalty upon thirty (30) days' written notice.

## 7. Confidentiality

7.1 Each party agrees to hold in confidence all Confidential Information received from the other party. ## 9. Termination

9.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement upon thirty (30) days' written notice if the other party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure such breach within the notice period.

9.2 Subscriber may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon sixty (60) days' written notice, provided that no refund of prepaid fees shall be due for the remaining term.

9.3 Provider may suspend Subscriber's access to the Service immediately if Subscriber (a) fails to pay fees when due after ten (10) days' written notice, or (b) uses the Service in violation of applicable law.

9.4 Upon termination, all rights granted to Subscriber under this Agreement shall immediately cease, and Subscriber shall discontinue all use of the Service.

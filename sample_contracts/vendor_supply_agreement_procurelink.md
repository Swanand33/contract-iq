# Vendor Supply Agreement

**Agreement Number:** PL-VSA-2024-0567  
**Effective Date:** February 1, 2024  
**Parties:**  
- **Buyer:** Cascade Manufacturing Inc., 5500 Industrial Way, Portland, OR 97209  
- **Vendor:** ProcureLink Supply Co., 3800 Distribution Center Road, Memphis, TN 38118  

---

## 1. Definitions

1.1 **"Products"** means the industrial components and materials described in the Product Schedule attached as Exhibit A.

1.2 **"Purchase Order" or "PO"** means a written order issued by Buyer for Products under this Agreement.

1.3 **"Specifications"** means the technical specifications, quality standards, and requirements for the Products as set forth in Exhibit B.

1.4 **"Lead Time"** means the number of business days from Vendor's receipt of a Purchase Order to shipment of the Products.

1.5 **"Minimum Order Quantity" or "MOQ"** means the minimum quantity of Products that may be ordered per Purchase Order as set forth in the Product Schedule.

---

## 2. Ordering and Delivery

2.1 Buyer shall submit Purchase Orders to Vendor by email or through Vendor's electronic ordering portal. Each PO shall specify: (a) Product description and part number; (b) quantity; (c) requested delivery date; (d) delivery address; and (e) any special handling instructions.

2.2 Vendor shall acknowledge receipt of each PO within two (2) business days and confirm or propose an alternative delivery date. Failure to respond within five (5) business days shall constitute acceptance of the PO.

2.3 Standard Lead Time is fifteen (15) business days. Expedited delivery may be available at an additional charge as agreed by the parties.

2.4 Vendor shall ship Products FOB Destination. Title and risk of loss transfer to Buyer upon delivery to the designated delivery address. Vendor shall provide tracking information for all shipments.

2.5 Buyer may reschedule or cancel a PO without penalty if written notice is provided at least ten (10) business days before the confirmed shipment date. Cancellations after this period may incur restocking fees up to fifteen percent (15%) of the PO value.

---

## 3. Pricing and Payment

3.1 Prices for Products shall be as set forth in the Product Schedule. Prices are firm for the first twelve (12) months of this Agreement.

3.2 After the initial twelve (12) month period, Vendor may propose price adjustments annually by providing at least sixty (60) days' written notice. Price increases shall not exceed five percent (5%) per year unless justified by documented increases in raw material costs exceeding ten percent (10%).

3.3 Vendor shall invoice Buyer upon shipment of Products. Payment terms are net forty-five (45) days from receipt of a correct and complete invoice.

3.4 Buyer may set off against invoices any amounts owed by Vendor to Buyer, including credits for returned or nonconforming Products.

3.5 All prices are exclusive of applicable sales, use, and excise taxes, which shall be separately stated on the invoice and borne by Buyer.

---

## 4. Quality and Inspection

4.1 All Products shall conform to the Specifications and be free from defects in materials and workmanship. Vendor shall maintain a quality management system certified to ISO 9001:2015 or equivalent.

4.2 Buyer shall have twenty (20) business days from receipt to inspect Products for conformity. Buyer shall notify Vendor of any nonconformity in writing, specifying the nature of the defect.

4.3 Vendor shall, at its option and expense, repair, replace, or credit Buyer for any nonconforming Products within fifteen (15) business days of notification. Return shipping for nonconforming Products shall be at Vendor's expense.

4.4 Acceptance of Products shall not relieve Vendor of liability for latent defects discovered after inspection, provided Buyer notifies Vendor within twelve (12) months of delivery.

4.5 Buyer or its authorized representative may audit Vendor's manufacturing facilities and quality systems upon thirty (30) days' prior written notice, not more than twice per year.

---

## 5. Warranty

5.1 Vendor warrants that all Products shall: (a) conform to the Specifications; (b) be free from defects in material and workmanship; (c) be merchantable and fit for their intended purpose; (d) comply with all applicable laws, regulations, and industry standards; and (e) be delivered free and clear of all liens and encumbrances.

5.2 The warranty period shall be twenty-four (24) months from delivery or eighteen (18) months from installation, whichever occurs first.

5.3 Vendor's warranty obligations include, at Buyer's election: repair, replacement, or full refund of the purchase price for defective Products, plus reimbursement of Buyer's reasonable costs of removal, inspection, and reinstallation.

---

## 6. Intellectual Property

6.1 Vendor retains all intellectual property rights in the Products and their design, except for custom Products designed to Buyer's specifications, which shall be owned by Buyer.

6.2 Vendor shall indemnify Buyer against any claim that the Products infringe any third-party patent, trademark, or other intellectual property right.

6.3 Neither party shall use the other party's trademarks, trade names, or logos without prior written consent.

---

## 7. Supply Assurance

7.1 Vendor shall maintain sufficient inventory and manufacturing capacity to fulfill Buyer's orders within the stated Lead Time.

7.2 If Vendor anticipates any inability to meet delivery commitments, Vendor shall notify Buyer within forty-eight (48) hours and provide: (a) the cause of the delay; (b) the expected duration; and (c) proposed mitigation measures.

7.3 Vendor shall maintain a business continuity plan and shall make a summary of such plan available to Buyer upon request.

7.4 If Vendor fails to deliver conforming Products within ten (10) business days of the confirmed delivery date, Buyer may, at its option: (a) accept late delivery; (b) source Products from an alternative vendor at Vendor's expense; or (c) cancel the PO without penalty.

---

## 8. Compliance

8.1 Vendor shall comply with all applicable federal, state, and local laws, regulations, and ordinances, including environmental regulations, labor laws, and workplace safety standards.

8.2 Vendor certifies that no Products supplied under this Agreement are produced using forced labor, child labor, or in violation of applicable human trafficking laws.

8.3 Vendor shall comply with Buyer's Supplier Code of Conduct, a copy of which has been provided to Vendor.

8.4 Vendor shall promptly notify Buyer of any recall, safety alert, or regulatory action affecting the Products.

---

## 9. Confidentiality

9.1 Each party shall maintain the confidentiality of the other party's confidential business information, including pricing, specifications, business plans, and customer information.

9.2 Confidentiality obligations survive termination for three (3) years.

---

## 10. Limitation of Liability

10.1 VENDOR'S TOTAL LIABILITY FOR ALL CLAIMS SHALL NOT EXCEED TWO TIMES THE ANNUAL PURCHASE VOLUME UNDER THIS AGREEMENT.

10.2 NEITHER PARTY SHALL BE LIABLE FOR CONSEQUENTIAL OR INDIRECT DAMAGES, EXCEPT FOR CLAIMS ARISING FROM VENDOR'S BREACH OF WARRANTY OR IP INFRINGEMENT.

---

## 11. Term and Termination

11.1 This Agreement shall have an initial term of two (2) years and shall automatically renew for additional one (1) year terms unless either party provides ninety (90) days' written notice of non-renewal.

11.2 Either party may terminate for cause upon thirty (30) days' written notice of material breach, if the breach is not cured within the notice period.

11.3 Buyer may terminate for convenience upon sixty (60) days' written notice. Vendor shall be entitled to payment for Products delivered and accepted, plus reasonable costs of work in progress.

11.4 Upon termination, Vendor shall fulfill all outstanding accepted POs unless otherwise agreed.

---

## 12. Governing Law

12.1 This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Oregon.

# Master Services Agreement

**Agreement Number:** BP-MSA-2024-0445  
**Effective Date:** April 15, 2024  
**Parties:**  
- **Client:** Atlas Global Trading Co., 1800 Harbor Boulevard, Long Beach, CA 90802  
- **Service Provider:** BrightPath Technology Services Inc., 600 Innovation Park, Atlanta, GA 30308  

---

## 1. Definitions

1.1 **"Services"** means the technology consulting, software development, and system integration services described in one or more Statements of Work.

1.2 **"SOW"** or **"Statement of Work"** means an executed document referencing this Agreement that describes the specific Services, Deliverables, timeline, milestones, and fees.

1.3 **"Deliverables"** means software, documentation, reports, designs, or other tangible outputs identified in a SOW.

1.4 **"Client Systems"** means Client's hardware, software, networks, and computing environments to which BrightPath may be granted access to perform the Services.

1.5 **"Personnel"** means BrightPath employees, contractors, and subcontractors assigned to perform Services.

1.6 **"Acceptance Criteria"** means the functional and performance requirements specified in a SOW against which Deliverables will be evaluated.

---

## 2. Engagement Structure

2.1 This Agreement establishes the general terms under which BrightPath will provide Services to Client. Specific engagements shall be governed by individual SOWs.

2.2 Each SOW shall specify: (a) scope of Services; (b) Deliverables and Acceptance Criteria; (c) project timeline and milestones; (d) BrightPath Personnel assigned; (e) fees and payment schedule; (f) Client responsibilities; and (g) any additional terms specific to the engagement.

2.3 In the event of a conflict between this Agreement and a SOW, the terms of the SOW shall prevail with respect to the specific engagement, except that the SOW may not diminish BrightPath's confidentiality obligations or Client's intellectual property rights as set forth herein.

---

## 3. Performance of Services

3.1 BrightPath shall perform the Services in a professional manner consistent with generally accepted industry standards and practices.

3.2 BrightPath shall assign Personnel with appropriate skills and experience. Key Personnel identified in a SOW shall not be removed or replaced without Client's prior written consent, except in cases of resignation, termination, or medical leave, in which case BrightPath shall provide a replacement of equal or greater qualifications within fifteen (15) business days.

3.3 BrightPath Personnel working at Client's facilities shall comply with Client's reasonable workplace policies, security procedures, and codes of conduct.

3.4 BrightPath shall provide weekly written status reports for each active SOW, including: progress against milestones, issues and risks, hours expended, and planned activities for the next period.

---

## 4. Client Responsibilities

4.1 Client shall: (a) provide timely access to Client Systems, data, and facilities as reasonably necessary for BrightPath to perform the Services; (b) designate a project manager with authority to make day-to-day decisions; (c) review and provide feedback on Deliverables within the timeframes specified in the SOW; and (d) make decisions on open items within five (5) business days of request, unless a different timeframe is specified.

4.2 Client acknowledges that delays in fulfilling Client's responsibilities may impact project timelines and milestones, and BrightPath shall be entitled to equitable adjustments to the timeline for any such delays.

---

## 5. Acceptance and Testing

5.1 BrightPath shall deliver each Deliverable to Client in accordance with the timeline in the SOW. Upon delivery, Client shall have fifteen (15) business days to test the Deliverable against the Acceptance Criteria (the "Acceptance Period").

5.2 If the Deliverable meets the Acceptance Criteria, Client shall provide written acceptance. If Client does not provide written acceptance or rejection within the Acceptance Period, the Deliverable shall be deemed accepted.

5.3 If the Deliverable does not meet the Acceptance Criteria, Client shall provide a detailed written description of the deficiencies. BrightPath shall correct the deficiencies and redeliver the Deliverable within ten (10) business days, and Client shall have an additional ten (10) business days to test.

5.4 If, after two (2) correction cycles, the Deliverable still does not meet the Acceptance Criteria, Client may: (a) accept the Deliverable with an equitable adjustment to fees; (b) extend the correction period by mutual agreement; or (c) terminate the applicable SOW and receive a refund of fees paid for the unaccepted Deliverable.

---

## 6. Fees and Payment

6.1 Client shall pay BrightPath the fees specified in each SOW. Fees may be structured as: (a) fixed-price for defined Deliverables; (b) time-and-materials at agreed hourly or daily rates; or (c) a hybrid of fixed-price milestones and time-and-materials phases.

6.2 BrightPath shall issue invoices as specified in each SOW. Client shall pay undisputed amounts within thirty (30) days of invoice date.

6.3 Client may dispute any invoice item in good faith by providing written notice within fifteen (15) days of receipt, along with a reasonable explanation. The parties shall work in good faith to resolve disputes within thirty (30) days.

6.4 Late payments on undisputed amounts shall bear interest at the lesser of 1.5% per month or the maximum rate permitted by law.

---

## 7. Intellectual Property

7.1 **Client Ownership.** All Deliverables created specifically for Client under a SOW shall be owned by Client upon full payment of the applicable fees. BrightPath hereby assigns all right, title, and interest in such Deliverables to Client.

7.2 **BrightPath Tools and Frameworks.** BrightPath retains ownership of its pre-existing tools, libraries, frameworks, and methodologies ("BrightPath Tools"). To the extent any Deliverable incorporates BrightPath Tools, BrightPath grants Client a non-exclusive, perpetual, royalty-free license to use such BrightPath Tools solely as integrated into the Deliverable.

7.3 **Residual Knowledge.** Nothing in this Agreement shall restrict BrightPath from using general knowledge, skills, and experience (including ideas, concepts, know-how, and techniques) gained during the performance of Services, provided that such use does not involve disclosure of Client's Confidential Information.

---

## 8. Confidentiality

8.1 "Confidential Information" means any non-public information disclosed by one party to the other, including business plans, financial data, customer information, trade secrets, source code, technical specifications, and the terms of this Agreement.

8.2 The Receiving Party shall: (a) use Confidential Information solely for purposes of this Agreement; (b) protect Confidential Information with at least the same care used for its own confidential information; and (c) limit disclosure to Personnel who need access and are bound by written confidentiality obligations.

8.3 BrightPath acknowledges that it may access Client's systems containing sensitive business data, including customer transaction records, supply chain information, and financial data. BrightPath shall implement appropriate access controls and shall not extract, copy, or retain such data except as specifically required to perform the Services.

8.4 Confidentiality obligations survive termination for five (5) years.

---

## 9. Representations and Warranties

9.1 BrightPath warrants that: (a) the Services will be performed in a workmanlike manner; (b) Deliverables will conform to the Acceptance Criteria for ninety (90) days following acceptance; (c) Deliverables will not infringe third-party intellectual property rights; and (d) BrightPath Personnel have appropriate qualifications and legal authorization to work.

9.2 EXCEPT AS SET FORTH ABOVE, BRIGHTPATH DISCLAIMS ALL OTHER WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED.

---

## 10. Indemnification

10.1 BrightPath shall indemnify Client against third-party claims that a Deliverable infringes any patent, copyright, or trade secret.

10.2 Client shall indemnify BrightPath against claims arising from Client's use of Deliverables in combination with non-BrightPath products or services, or Client's modification of Deliverables.

---

## 11. Limitation of Liability

11.1 NEITHER PARTY'S AGGREGATE LIABILITY SHALL EXCEED THE TOTAL FEES PAID OR PAYABLE UNDER THE SOW GIVING RISE TO THE CLAIM.

11.2 NEITHER PARTY SHALL BE LIABLE FOR INDIRECT, CONSEQUENTIAL, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES.

11.3 These limitations do not apply to breaches of confidentiality, IP infringement indemnification, or willful misconduct.

---

## 12. Term and Termination

12.1 This Agreement shall remain in effect for three (3) years from the Effective Date and shall automatically renew for successive one (1) year terms unless terminated by either party with ninety (90) days' written notice.

12.2 Either party may terminate for material breach with thirty (30) days' written notice and opportunity to cure.

12.3 Client may terminate any SOW for convenience with thirty (30) days' written notice. Client shall pay for all Services performed through termination plus reasonable wind-down costs.

12.4 Upon termination, BrightPath shall deliver all work in progress and Client Materials. Sections 7, 8, 10, 11, and 13 survive termination.

---

## 13. General Provisions

13.1 **Governing Law.** This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Georgia.

13.2 **Independent Contractor.** BrightPath is an independent contractor.

13.3 **Non-Solicitation.** Neither party shall solicit the other's employees involved in the Services for twelve (12) months after the last SOW completion.

13.4 **Force Majeure.** Neither party shall be liable for delays caused by events beyond reasonable control.

13.5 **Entire Agreement.** This Agreement and all SOWs constitute the complete agreement between the parties.
